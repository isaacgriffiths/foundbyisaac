Deleting your data

Crossfeed · last updated 20 August 2026

You can delete your Crossfeed data at any time. There are two ways.

Disconnect a single platform

  1. Open Crossfeed and go to Settings.
  2. Tap the connected account you want to remove.
  3. Tap Disconnect.

This revokes our access to that platform and deletes the stored access token straight away.

Delete your whole account

  1. Open Crossfeed and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, every connected platform token and every metric we have recorded. It cannot be undone.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, write to the address below from the email address you signed up with, asking for deletion. We will confirm once it is done, normally within 30 days.

Removing Crossfeed from Facebook or Instagram

You can also revoke access from the platform's side, at Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, or Instagram Settings → Apps and websites. Doing so stops any further data being collected; use one of the routes above to delete what has already been stored.
